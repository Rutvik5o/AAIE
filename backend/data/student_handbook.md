# Student Handbook 2026–27

## About This Handbook and Northbridge Institute

This handbook sets out the official student policies of Northbridge Institute of Management & Technology (a fictional institution) for the academic year 2026–27. It applies to all students enrolled in the MBA, PGDM, BBA, B.Tech CSE and M.Tech Data Science programmes. The academic year is divided into three terms: Term 1 (July to October), Term 2 (November to February) and Term 3 (March to June). All amounts in this handbook are in Indian Rupees (INR). Where this handbook and a programme-specific notice differ, the more recent notice issued by the Registrar's Office applies. Students are expected to read this handbook at the start of each academic year and to check the student portal regularly for updates. Questions about any policy can be raised through the support ticket system described in the section on raising a support request.

## Fee Structure and Fee Components

Each term's fee is made up of several components, all shown separately on the fee statement in the student portal. The tuition fee covers teaching, course materials and access to the learning management system. The academic services fee covers examinations, library access, laboratory use and career services. The technology fee covers campus Wi-Fi, software licences and the student email account. A one-time caution deposit of ₹10,000 is collected in Term 1 of the first year and is refundable on graduation or withdrawal after deducting any dues. Indicative term fees for 2026–27 are: MBA and PGDM ₹1,85,000; M.Tech Data Science ₹1,40,000; B.Tech CSE ₹1,10,000; BBA ₹75,000. The total payable for a term is the sum of all components minus any approved scholarship adjustment. Optional charges, such as elective study tours, are billed separately and are not part of the term fee.

## Fee Payment Deadlines

Fees must be paid by the published due date for each term. For 2026–27 the deadlines are: Term 1 fees due 15 July 2026, Term 2 fees due 15 November 2026, and Term 3 fees due 15 March 2027. A student is considered to have paid on time only when the full term amount (or the scheduled instalment, if on an approved instalment plan) has been received by the Finance Office on or before the due date. Fee statements are published on the student portal at least 30 days before each due date, and reminder emails are sent to the student's institute email address 14 days and 3 days before the deadline. Not receiving a reminder email does not extend the deadline. Students who expect difficulty paying should apply for an instalment plan before the due date rather than after it.

## Accepted Payment Methods

Fees can be paid through the official online fee payment portal using net banking, UPI, or debit and credit cards issued by Indian banks. Card payments may attract a convenience charge levied by the payment gateway, which is shown before the payment is confirmed. Fees can also be paid by NEFT or RTGS transfer to the Institute's bank account; the account details are printed on the fee statement, and the student ID must be entered in the transfer remarks. Demand drafts in favour of "Northbridge Institute of Management & Technology" are accepted at the Finance Office counter during office hours. Cash payments are not accepted for fees above ₹10,000. Education loan disbursements paid directly by a bank to the Institute are accepted, but the student remains responsible for ensuring the amount reaches the Institute by the due date.

## Payment Portal Failures on the Due Date

If the online fee payment portal fails or is unavailable on the due date, the student will not be penalised for the outage. In this case the student may pay on the next working day without any late fee, provided that the student reports the failure within 24 hours of the failed attempt. The report must be made either by raising a support ticket in the Finance category or by emailing finance@northbridge.edu, and it must include a screenshot or the exact error message shown, together with the date and time of the attempt. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays notified by the Institute. For example, if the due date falls on a Friday and the portal fails, payment must be completed by the following Monday (or Tuesday if Monday is a public holiday). If the failure is not reported within 24 hours, normal late payment fees apply.

## Late Payment Fees and Registration Block

Fees not received by the due date attract a late payment fee of ₹500 per calendar day, starting the day after the due date. The total late fee for any one term is capped at ₹7,500. The late fee is added to the student's outstanding balance and appears on the fee statement in the student portal. If fees remain unpaid more than 15 days after the due date, the student's registration for the term is blocked. A blocked student cannot add or drop courses, access the learning management system, or receive hall tickets for examinations until all dues, including late fees, are cleared. The block is lifted automatically within one working day of full payment. Late fees may be waived only in the case of a reported portal failure (see the section on payment portal failures) or with written approval from the Finance Controller for documented emergencies.

## Instalment Plans

Students who cannot pay the full term fee at once may apply for an instalment plan. Under the standard plan, the term fee is split into two instalments: 60% due on the regular due date and 40% due 45 days later. Applications must be submitted through the student portal under Finance > Instalment Request at least 7 days before the term due date, and must include a short statement of reasons. Approval is at the discretion of the Finance Office and is usually communicated within 3 working days. An instalment plan is not available to students who already have overdue fees from a previous term. If an instalment is missed, the plan is cancelled and the full remaining balance becomes due immediately, with late fees calculated from the original instalment date. Only one instalment plan may be approved per academic year.

## Refunds and Withdrawal

A student who withdraws from the Institute must submit the withdrawal form WD-1 to the Registrar's Office. Tuition refunds depend on the date the completed form is received: withdrawal before the term start date receives a 100% refund of tuition minus a ₹5,000 processing fee; withdrawal within the first two weeks of term receives 75%; withdrawal in weeks three and four receives 50%; no tuition refund is given after week four. The technology fee and academic services fee are not refundable once the term has started. The caution deposit is refunded in full minus any outstanding dues, library fines or damages. Refunds are paid only to the bank account from which the fee was paid, or to the student's verified bank account, within 30 working days of approval. Scholarship amounts adjusted against fees are not refunded to the student.

## Fee Receipts and Statements

A digital fee receipt is generated automatically for every successful payment and can be downloaded from the student portal under Finance > Receipts. Receipts for online payments are normally available within 2 hours; receipts for NEFT, RTGS and demand draft payments are issued within 3 working days of the funds being credited. Each receipt shows the student ID, term, amount, payment method, transaction reference and date. The consolidated fee statement for the academic year, often required by banks for education loans or by employers for reimbursement, can also be downloaded from the portal. If a payment has been debited from the student's account but no receipt appears after 3 working days, the student should raise a Finance support ticket with the transaction reference. Duplicate stamped receipts can be requested from the Finance Office at no charge.

## Scholarships Overview and Types

The Institute offers three main categories of scholarship. Merit Scholarships reward strong academic performance and are awarded on the basis of entrance scores for first-year students and CGPA for continuing students; the amount ranges from ₹25,000 to ₹75,000 per year. Need-Based Scholarships support students whose annual family income is below ₹8,00,000 and cover between 25% and 50% of tuition. Sports Scholarships are awarded to students who have represented their state or country in a recognised sport and who commit to representing the Institute in inter-college events; these are worth up to ₹40,000 per year. A student may hold only one Institute scholarship at a time, but may also receive external scholarships from government or private bodies. All scholarships are reviewed annually and are not automatically renewed.

## Scholarship Eligibility and Renewal Criteria

To be eligible for any Institute scholarship, a student must be enrolled full-time, have no disciplinary action recorded in the current year, and have no overdue fees at the time of application. Merit Scholarship renewal requires a minimum CGPA of 7.5 at the end of the previous academic year and at least 75% attendance in every course. Need-Based Scholarship renewal requires updated income documents each year and a minimum CGPA of 6.0. Sports Scholarship renewal requires a minimum CGPA of 6.0 and a satisfactory participation report from the Sports Committee. A student whose CGPA falls below the required level loses the scholarship for the following year but may reapply once the criteria are met again. Students found to have submitted false information will have the scholarship cancelled and must repay any amounts already adjusted.

## Scholarship Application Process and Deadlines

Scholarship applications are submitted online through the student portal under Scholarships > Apply. Applicants must upload the relevant documents: mark sheets for merit applications, family income certificates and the latest income tax return for need-based applications, and sports certificates for sports applications. For 2026–27, the application window for continuing students runs from 1 August to 15 September 2026; first-year students apply at the time of admission. The Scholarship Committee reviews applications in two rounds, and results are published on the portal by 31 October for the first round and by 30 November for the second round. While an application is being assessed, its status is shown as "under review". Incomplete applications are not considered. Students may appeal a rejection within 10 working days of the decision by writing to scholarships@northbridge.edu.

## Scholarship Disbursement

Approved Institute scholarships are not paid to students in cash. Instead, the scholarship amount is adjusted against the student's fees, normally split equally across the remaining terms of the academic year. The adjustment appears as a credit line on the fee statement, and the student pays only the balance. If a scholarship is approved after the student has already paid the full term fee, the credit is carried forward to the next term; any excess at the end of the academic year is refunded to the student's verified bank account. External scholarships paid to the Institute by a third party are credited in the same way once the funds are received. While a scholarship is under review, the student must pay fees as normal by the due date; the scholarship credit will be applied once approved. Scholarships are withdrawn if the student withdraws from the programme.

## Course Add/Drop Window

Students may add or drop courses during the add/drop window, which is the first two weeks (14 calendar days) of each term. All changes are made online through the student portal under Academics > Course Registration. Adding a course is subject to seat availability and to any prerequisites listed in the course catalogue. Dropping a course within the window does not appear on the transcript and has no fee consequence. Students must keep the minimum credit load for their programme after any drop; the portal will not allow a change that takes a student below the minimum. Changes take effect immediately, and the student's timetable is updated within one working day. Students are advised to attend the first sessions of a course before deciding. Students with a registration block due to unpaid fees cannot use the add/drop function until the block is lifted.

## Elective or Course Change After the Deadline

After the add/drop window has closed, a student may change an elective or course only in exceptional circumstances, such as a timetable clash, a medical reason, or a course being cancelled by the Institute. The student must submit the Course Change Form CC-2, available on the portal under Academics > Forms, with a written justification. The request requires approval from the Programme Director, who may consult the faculty teaching both courses. A non-refundable processing fee of ₹1,000 applies to every approved late course change; the fee is waived if the change is caused by the Institute cancelling a course. Requests are normally decided within 5 working days. Late changes are not permitted after week six of the term. A course dropped after the deadline may appear on the transcript as "W" (withdrawn).

## Programme Transfer

Students may apply to transfer from one programme to another only at the end of an academic year. Transfers are allowed only between programmes at the same level (undergraduate to undergraduate, postgraduate to postgraduate) with compatible entry requirements; for example, a transfer from PGDM to MBA may be considered, but a transfer from BBA to B.Tech CSE is not permitted. The application must be submitted to the Registrar's Office by 31 May, with a statement of purpose and the consent of the current Programme Director. The receiving programme's admissions committee decides whether credits already earned will be recognised. A student may transfer programmes only once during their studies. If approved, the fee for the new programme applies from the following term, and any scholarship is reassessed under the new programme's rules. Mid-year transfers are not permitted.

## Attendance Requirements

Students must attend at least 75% of the scheduled sessions in each course to be eligible to sit the end-term examination for that course. Attendance is recorded electronically in every session and can be checked on the student portal under Academics > Attendance. Students whose attendance falls below 80% receive an automatic warning email. Absence due to documented medical reasons, approved academic leave or official representation of the Institute (for example in sports or competitions) may be condoned up to a further 10%, on submission of evidence to the Programme Office within 7 days of returning. A student who does not meet the attendance requirement is marked "Debarred" for that course and must re-register for it in a later term, paying the applicable course fee. Repeated low attendance may also affect scholarship renewal.

## Examinations, Results and Re-evaluation

Each course has continuous assessment components and an end-term examination; the weightage for each course is published in the course outline. Hall tickets for end-term examinations are issued on the portal one week before exams, and only to students with no registration block. Results are published on the portal within 21 days of the last examination. A student who is not satisfied with a grade may apply for re-evaluation of an end-term answer script within 7 days of results being published, by submitting a request through Academics > Re-evaluation and paying ₹750 per course. The script is re-marked by a different examiner, and the revised mark is final, whether higher or lower. If the grade improves, the re-evaluation fee is refunded. A supplementary examination is offered for failed courses at the start of the following term for a fee of ₹1,500 per course.

## Academic Leave

A student may apply for academic leave of absence for medical, family or personal reasons. Short leave of up to 10 working days is approved by the Programme Office and requires an application through the portal with supporting documents. Long leave of one full term or more is approved by the Dean of Academics and requires the Leave Form AL-1. A student on approved long leave does not pay tuition for the term of absence, but must pay a ₹5,000 registration retention fee to keep their place. The maximum total academic leave during a programme is two terms. On returning, the student rejoins the programme at the point of departure and must follow the curriculum in force at that time. Leave does not extend scholarship eligibility, and scholarship credits are paused during long leave.

## Raising a Support Request

Students can raise a support request (ticket) through the student portal under Help > New Ticket, or by asking the AI Student Support Agent to create one. Each ticket must have a category: Finance (fees, payments, receipts, refunds), Academics (courses, exams, attendance), Scholarships, IT (Wi-Fi, email, portal login, software), Hostel and Facilities, or General. Every ticket receives a reference number in the format TKT-XXXX, which the student should quote in any follow-up. Target first-response times are: Finance and Scholarships within 2 working days; Academics within 2 working days; IT within 1 working day; Hostel and Facilities within 1 working day; General within 3 working days. Tickets marked Urgent, for example a portal failure on a fee due date or an issue preventing exam access, receive a first response within 4 hours during office hours. Students can track ticket status on the portal.

## Student Data Privacy and Security

The Institute processes student data in line with applicable Indian data protection law and uses it only for academic, administrative and support purposes. Students can only view their own records; staff and support systems will not disclose one student's fee, scholarship, grade or personal information to another student. Institute staff, including the AI Student Support Agent, will never ask for a student's password, OTP (one-time password), full card number, CVV or UPI PIN. Students must not share these details in emails, chats or support tickets; if they are shared by mistake, they will not be stored, and the student should change the password or contact their bank immediately. Sensitive information such as health details is recorded only when needed for an approved process, such as academic leave. Students may request a copy of their personal data from the Data Protection Officer at privacy@northbridge.edu.
